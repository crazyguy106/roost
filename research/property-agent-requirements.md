# Singapore Property Agent Compliance & Execution Reference (2024-2025)

## Executive Summary
This practitioner-grade reference document serves as the authoritative source-of-truth for Singapore Real Estate Salespersons (RES) regarding regulatory compliance, contractual execution, and workflow automation. It incorporates the latest 2024-2025 legislative updates, including the transition to the 16-hour CPD framework, the mandatory HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter rollout, and enhanced AML/CFT screening requirements under the 2025 Anti-Money Laundering Act. The document is designed for both training purposes and as a blueprint for RPA/AI automation in real estate operations.

## 1. Regulatory Framework

### 1.1 Primary Legislation & Oversight
The Singapore real estate industry is governed by the **Estate Agents Act 2010**, which established the **Council for Estate Agencies (CEA)** as the statutory board under the Ministry of National Development (MND). 
- **Key Statute**: Estate Agents Act 2010.
- **Recent Amendment**: The **Anti-Money Laundering and Other Matters (Estate Agents and Developers) Act 2025** introduced stricter penalties for non-compliance and enhanced oversight of transaction records.
- **Operational Manual**: The **Professional Service Manual (PSM)** (latest update: August 2025) serves as the definitive guide for ethical conduct and professional standards.
- **Code of Ethics**: The **Code of Ethics and Professional Client Care (CEPCC)** is a schedule under the Estate Agents (Estate Agency Work) Regulations 2010, outlining mandatory behaviors (e.g., honesty, transparency, and no dual representation).

### 1.2 Salesperson Licensing & Registration
To practice as a Real Estate Salesperson (RES), individuals must be registered with a licensed Estate Agency (EA).
- **Requirements**: 21+ years old, 4 GCE ‘O’ Level passes (or WPLN Level 5), and a pass in the **RES Examination**.
- **Registration Portal**: All applications are processed via the **ACEAS (Advanced CEA Estate Agencies System)**.
- **Returning Agents**: Salespersons who have been out of the industry for more than 24 months must retake the RES course and examination before re-registration.

### 1.3 CPD Requirements & Renewal Cycle
The Continuing Professional Development (CPD) framework ensures agents stay updated on laws and market trends.
- **Current Cycle (Transition)**: The 2024–2025 cycle has been extended to **31 December 2025**.
- **Requirement (Until 31 Dec 2025)**: 6 CPD credits (4 Professional, 2 Generic).
- **New Requirement (From 1 Jan 2026)**: **16 training hours** annually, consisting of:
    - 12 hours of Structured Learning (including 4 hours of "Prescribed Essentials" on Ethics/Laws).
    - 4 hours of Self-directed Learning.
- **Renewal Period**: Annual (typically October 1 to November 30).
- **Fees**: Total renewal fee is approximately **S$283.50** (S$230 registration + S$53.50 application fee, inclusive of GST). *Source: CEA ACEAS Portal Fee Schedule.*

### 1.4 Key CEA Notices and Practice Circulars (Active)
CEA issues two distinct instrument types — both are binding on registered salespersons but carry different weight:
- **Notices / Practice Guidelines**: mandatory directives issued under the Estate Agents Act. Non-compliance is a disciplinary offence.
- **Practice Circulars (PC)**: interpretive guidance and reminders. Persuasive authority; cited in disciplinary cases as the expected standard of conduct.

Notable active instruments:
- **Notice 19-25**: Implementation of enhanced AML/CFT measures.
- **Notice 24-25**: Mandatory monthly transaction record submission via ACEAS.
- **PC 01-21 & 02-23**: Detailed AML/CFT guidance, including Customer Due Diligence (CDD).
- **PC 01-20**: Mandatory identity checks for all tenants/occupiers under the Women's Charter.
- **PC 01-11**: Mandatory use of Prescribed Estate Agency Agreement Forms.

## 2. Mandatory Documents and Forms

### 2.1 Prescribed CEA Estate Agency Agreements
Under the Estate Agents Act, agents **must** use the prescribed forms for all residential transactions. These forms protect both the agent and the client by clearly defining the scope of work and commission.
- **Form 1 & 2**: Sale of Residential Property (Non-Exclusive / Exclusive).
- **Form 3 & 4**: Purchase of Residential Property (Non-Exclusive / Exclusive).
- **Form 5 & 6**: Lease of Residential Property by Landlord (Non-Exclusive / Exclusive).
- **Form 7 & 8**: Lease of Residential Property by Tenant (Non-Exclusive / Exclusive).
- **Validity**: Exclusive agreements are valid for a maximum of **3 months** and must be renewed in writing.

### 2.2 Transaction-Specific Document Sequences
#### HDB Resale (Modern HFE Flow)
1.  **HFE (HDB Flat Eligibility) Letter**: Buyer must obtain this *before* the seller grants an OTP. Valid for 9 months.
2.  **Intent to Sell**: Seller must register this on the HDB Flat Portal at least 7 days before granting an OTP.
3.  **Option to Purchase (OTP)**: A prescribed HDB form with a unique serial number. Option Fee: S$1 to S$1,000.
4.  **Valuation Request**: Buyer must request valuation by the next working day after the OTP date if using CPF/loan.
5.  **Resale Checklist**: Both parties complete this on the HDB portal.
6.  **Exercise OTP**: Buyer pays Exercise Fee (Total deposit max S$5,000) within 21 calendar days.
7.  **Resale Application**: Both parties submit their portions within 7 days of each other.

#### Private Resale
1.  **Estate Agency Agreement**: Signed before marketing.
2.  **Option to Purchase (OTP)**: Usually a standard firm-specific or Law Society template. Option Fee: Typically 1%.
3.  **Exercise OTP**: Buyer pays Exercise Fee (Typically 4%) within 14 days (negotiable).
4.  **Sale & Purchase (S&P) Agreement**: Executed upon exercise.

#### Rental
1.  **Letter of Intent (LOI)**: Optional; outlines terms and "good faith" deposit.
2.  **Tenancy Agreement (TA)**: CEA provides a standard template, though agencies often use custom versions.
3.  **Inventory List**: Detailed list of furniture/fittings, signed by both parties at handover.

### 2.3 Compliance & Tax Forms
- **Customer's Particulars Form**: Mandatory for AML/CFT (CDD) for all clients.
- **Stamp Duty Certificates**: Issued by IRAS after payment of BSD/ABSD/SSD.
- **SLA LDAU Approval**: Required for non-Singaporeans purchasing landed property.
- **Resale Checklist for Sellers/Buyers**: Mandatory for HDB transactions to ensure all policies (MOP, EIP) are understood.

## 3. Standard Clauses Agents Must Understand

### 3.1 Option to Purchase (OTP) Clauses
- **Explanation**: A contract where the seller gives the buyer the exclusive right to buy the property at a fixed price within a specific timeframe.
- **Legal Basis**: Contract Law / HDB Regulations.
- **Agent Error**: Failing to ensure the HDB OTP is exercised within exactly 21 calendar days (by 4 PM on the expiry date). For private property, failing to specify if the option fee is refundable if the deal falls through due to specific conditions.

### 3.2 Cooling-Off Period (New Launches)
- **Explanation**: Buyers of uncompleted private residential property from developers have a 3-week window to exercise the OTP after receiving the S&P Agreement.
- **Legal Basis**: Housing Developers (Control and Licensing) Act.
- **Agent Error**: Not informing the buyer that if they back out, the developer forfeits **25% of the booking fee** (usually 1.25% of the purchase price).

### 3.3 Cooling Measures (ABSD/SSD)
- **Explanation**: Taxes designed to stabilize the market. **ABSD** (Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty) varies by residency and property count. **SSD** (Seller's Stamp Duty) applies if the property is sold within 3 years.
- **Legal Basis**: Stamp Duties Act.
- **Current ABSD rates (effective 27 Apr 2023):**
  - Singapore Citizens: **0% / 20% / 30%** (1st / 2nd / 3rd+ residential property)
  - Singapore PRs: **5% / 30% / 35%** (1st / 2nd / 3rd+)
  - Foreigners: **60%** flat (any residential purchase)
  - Entities: **65%** flat
  - Trustees: **65%** flat (with possible remission under qualifying living-trust conditions)
- **Current SSD rates (holding period from purchase):** 12% (≤1 yr) / 8% (>1–2 yrs) / 4% (>2–3 yrs) / 0% (>3 yrs).
- **Agent Error**: Quoting stale ABSD rates (the pre-Apr-2023 PR-2nd rate of 25% is now 30%; foreigner rate jumped from 30% to 60%); failing to warn a seller about the 12% SSD if they sell within the first year.

### 3.4 Estate Agency Agreement (EAA) Clauses
- **Explanation**: Clauses covering exclusivity, commission rates, and co-broking.
- **Legal Basis**: Estate Agents Act.
- **Agent Error**: Not disclosing a conflict of interest (e.g., the agent is related to the buyer) in writing, which is a breach of the CEPCC.

### 3.5 Subject-To (Contingency) Clauses
- **Explanation**: Clauses that make the sale contingent on certain events, such as "Subject to Financing" or "Subject to Sale of Current Property."
- **Legal Basis**: Contractual agreement.
- **Agent Error**: Using vague language like "subject to bank loan" without specifying the loan amount, interest rate, or the deadline for the Letter of Offer.

### 3.6 HDB-Specific Clauses
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP)**: Owners must live in the flat for 5 years before selling. *Error*: Marketing a flat before the MOP is met without HDB's written approval.
- **Ethnic Integration Policy (EIP)**: Quotas to ensure ethnic diversity. *Error*: Granting an OTP without checking the monthly updated EIP quota on the HDB website.

### 3.7 Tenancy Agreement Clauses
- **Diplomatic Clause**: Allows expatriate tenants to terminate the lease early (usually after 12 months) if they are transferred out of Singapore.
- **Reinstatement Clause**: Requires the tenant to return the property in its original condition. *Error*: Not taking detailed "before" photos, leading to security deposit disputes.

### 3.8 AML/CFT Compliance Clauses
- **Explanation**: Clauses allowing the agent to terminate the relationship if the client fails to provide ID or if suspicious activity is detected.
- **Legal Basis**: PC 01-21 / PC 02-23.
- **Agent Error**: "Tipping off" a client that they are being investigated for money laundering, which is a criminal offense.

## 4. Compliance Workflows

### 4.1 CEA Conduct & Record-Keeping
- **Obligation**: Maintain all transaction documents, including agreements, forms, and correspondence.
- **Time Window**: **5 years** from the date of the transaction.
- **Documentation**: Signed CEA forms, OTP, S&P, TA, and AML screening results.
- **Automation**: Auto-archiving of signed DocuSign files to a secure, searchable cloud storage system.

### 4.2 AML/CFT Onboarding & Screening
- **Obligation**: Verify client identity and screen against MAS/UN sanctions lists.
- **Time Window**: Before signing the Estate Agency Agreement or granting an OTP.
- **Documentation**: Customer's Particulars Form + Screening Report.
- **Automation**: AI-powered screening that cross-references NRIC/Passport data with global sanctions APIs (e.g., Sentro).

### 4.3 PDPA & DNC Compliance
- **Obligation**: Check the DNC Registry before sending marketing messages.
- **Time Window**: Every **21 days**.
- **Documentation**: DNC search results/logs.
- **Automation**: RPA bot that automatically scrubs lead lists against the PDPC portal every 21 days.

### 4.4 Conflict of Interest & Dual Representation
- **Obligation**: Disclose any personal interest in a transaction and avoid representing both sides.
- **Time Window**: Immediate disclosure upon identifying a conflict.
- **Documentation**: Written disclosure form signed by the client.
- **Automation**: CRM flag that alerts the agent if a client's name matches a known relative or associate in the database.

### 4.5 Stamp Duty Filing & Deadlines
- **Obligation**: File and pay BSD, ABSD, or SSD.
- **Time Window**: **14 days** for local documents; **30 days** for documents signed overseas.
- **Documentation**: Stamp Certificate from IRAS.
- **Automation**: AI extraction of data from the S&P Agreement to pre-fill the IRAS e-Stamping portal.

### 4.6 HDB Resale Submission Flow
- **Obligation**: Ensure all steps (HFE, Intent to Sell, OTP, Valuation) are completed in sequence.
- **Time Window**: 21 days for OTP; 7 days for resale application submission.
- **Documentation**: HFE Letter, OTP, Resale Application status.
- **Automation**: Workflow tracker that monitors HFE expiry and sends automated reminders to the agent and client.

### 4.7 Foreigner & PR Purchase Rules (SLA LDAU)
- **Obligation**: Ensure non-citizens obtain SLA approval for landed property.
- **Time Window**: Before exercising the OTP.
- **Documentation**: SLA Approval Letter.
- **Automation**: Automated check of the buyer's residency status against SLA's "restricted property" criteria.

### 4.8 Dispute Resolution & Mediation
- **Obligation**: Refer disputes to CEA-approved mediation centers if unresolved.
- **Time Window**: As per the CEA Dispute Resolution Scheme.
- **Documentation**: Mediation agreement or SMC award.
- **Automation**: Case management system to track client complaints and resolution timelines.

## 6. High-Leverage Automation Candidates

1.  **The "HFE Guardian" (AI Agent)**:
    *   *Trigger*: Client uploads HFE letter.
    *   *Steps*: Extract expiry date via OCR -> Calculate 30-day and 7-day warnings -> Sync to agent's calendar.
    *   *Output*: Automated WhatsApp/Email alerts to client. *Time Saved*: 1 hour/deal.
2.  **"Instant CDD" (RPA)**:
    *   *Trigger*: Upload of NRIC/Passport photo.
    *   *Steps*: OCR extraction -> Search MAS/UN/Interpol lists via API -> Generate PDF Compliance Report.
    *   *Output*: Ready-to-file CDD record. *Time Saved*: 30 mins/client.
3.  **"DNC Scrub-Bot" (RPA)**:
    *   *Trigger*: New lead list uploaded to CRM.
    *   *Steps*: Login to PDPC portal -> Batch check numbers -> Tag "Do Not Call" in CRM.
    *   *Output*: Cleaned calling list. *Time Saved*: 2 hours/week.
4.  **"Stamp Duty Forecaster" (AI Agent)**:
    *   *Trigger*: Input of price and buyer profile.
    *   *Steps*: Apply latest tiered BSD/ABSD rates -> Check for remission eligibility (e.g., FTA countries).
    *   *Output*: Precise tax breakdown for financial planning. *Time Saved*: 20 mins/consultation.
5.  **"MOP Tracker"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Completion date of a past HDB purchase.
    *   *Steps*: Calculate 5-year MOP -> Set reminder for 6 months before expiry.
    *   *Output*: Lead generation alert for the agent. *Time Saved*: Continuous lead gen.
6.  **"EIP Quota Checker"**:
    *   *Trigger*: New listing address entered.
    *   *Steps*: Scrape HDB EIP/SPR quota page for the specific block/month.
    *   *Output*: Status report (Quota Available/Closed). *Time Saved*: 15 mins/listing.
7.  **"Inventory List Generator"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Upload of property photos.
    *   *Steps*: AI vision analysis to identify furniture -> Generate structured list.
    *   *Output*: Draft Inventory List for TA. *Time Saved*: 1 hour/rental.
8.  **"Stamp Duty Filing Assistant"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Signed S&P uploaded.
    *   *Steps*: OCR extraction -> Map to IRAS e-Stamping fields.
    *   *Output*: Pre-filled draft for IRAS portal. *Time Saved*: 45 mins/filing.
9.  **"Conflict of Interest Disclosure Bot"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Agent enters their own name in a transaction.
    *   *Steps*: Flag conflict -> Generate CEA-compliant disclosure form.
    *   *Output*: Signed disclosure record. *Time Saved*: 15 mins/incident.
10. **"Co-broking Agreement Generator"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Verbal agreement with co-broke agent.
    *   *Steps*: Collect CEA number -> Generate commission split agreement -> Send via DocuSign.
    *   *Output*: Legally binding contract. *Time Saved*: 30 mins/deal.
11. **"SLA Title Search Automator"**:
    *   *Trigger*: New listing agreement signed.
    *   *Steps*: Login to SLA INLIS -> Purchase property info -> Extract owner names.
    *   *Output*: Verification report. *Time Saved*: 30 mins/listing.
12. **"CPD Hour Logger"**:
    *   *Trigger*: Course certificate received in email.
    *   *Steps*: Parse certificate -> Update internal tracker -> Compare against CEA requirement.
    *   *Output*: Dashboard showing renewal readiness. *Time Saved*: 1 hour/year.
