### Expanded Findings on Clauses & Workflows (Synthesized from Grounding & Official Frameworks)

- **Subject-to-Sale Clause**:
    - *Explanation*: Contingency where the buyer's obligation to complete the purchase depends on the successful sale of their current property.
    - *Legal Basis*: Contractual (Common Law).
    - *Agent Error*: Vague timelines; failing to define "successful sale" (e.g., OTP signed vs. OTP exercised vs. Completion).
- **Subject-to-Financing Clause**:
    - *Explanation*: Buyer can rescind the contract if they fail to secure a bank loan of a specified amount by a certain date.
    - *Legal Basis*: Contractual.
    - *Agent Error*: Not specifying the loan amount or the "reasonable efforts" required by the buyer to obtain the loan.
- **HDB MOP (Minimum Occupation Period)**:
    - *Explanation*: 5-year period (usually) where owners must live in the flat before selling or renting out the whole unit.
    - *Legal Basis*: Housing and Development Act.
    - *Agent Error*: Miscalculating MOP start date (it starts from the date of keys collection, not OTP).
- **HDB EIP (Ethnic Integration Policy) & SPR Quota**:
    - *Explanation*: Quotas to ensure ethnic diversity and manage the concentration of Singapore Permanent Residents.
    - *Legal Basis*: HDB Policy.
    - *Agent Error*: Failing to check the monthly updated quota on the HDB website before the OTP is granted.
- **Foreigner Landed Property (SLA LDAU)**:
    - *Explanation*: Non-Singapore Citizens must apply for approval from the Land Dealings Approval Unit (LDAU) to purchase "restricted" residential property (landed).
    - *Legal Basis*: Residential Property Act.
    - *Agent Error*: Proceeding with OTP without advising the buyer to obtain "Approval-in-Principle" from SLA.
- **Dual Representation Prohibition**:
    - *Explanation*: An agent cannot represent both buyer and seller (or landlord and tenant) in the same transaction.
    - *Legal Basis*: Estate Agents (Estate Agency Work) Regulations 2010.
    - *Agent Error*: Collecting commission from both sides; failing to disclose a conflict of interest in writing.
- **Stamp Duty Deadlines**:
    - *Explanation*: 14 days from the date of the S&P/OTP (if exercised) for local documents; 30 days for documents signed overseas.
    - *Legal Basis*: Stamp Duties Act.
    - *Agent Error*: Missing the 14-day window, leading to penalties (up to 4x the duty).
- **PDPA DNC (Do Not Call)**:
    - *Explanation*: Agents must check the DNC Registry before sending telemarketing messages unless they have "clear and unambiguous consent" in written or other accessible form.
    - *Legal Basis*: Personal Data Protection Act.
    - *Agent Error*: Relying on "implied consent" from a previous transaction that has ended.
- **HDB Resale Timeline (Modern HFE Process)**:
    - *Step 1: HFE Letter*: Buyer must have a valid HFE letter before the Seller can grant an OTP. Validity: 9 months.
    - *Step 2: OTP*: Seller grants OTP. Option Fee: S$1 to S$1,000.
    - *Step 3: Valuation*: Buyer (if using CPF/loan) must request HDB valuation by the next working day after the OTP date.
    - *Step 4: Exercise*: Buyer exercises OTP within 21 calendar days. Exercise Fee: Max S$4,000 (Total deposit max S$5,000).
    - *Step 5: Submission*: Both parties submit resale application within 7 days of each other.
    - *Step 6: Completion*: Approx. 8 weeks after HDB's acceptance of the application.
- **Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) Tiers (Residential)**:
    - First S$180,000: 1%
    - Next S$180,000: 2%
    - Next S$640,000: 3%
    - Next S$500,000: 4%
    - Next S$1,500,000: 5%
    - Remaining amount (>S$3,000,000): 6%
    - *Source: IRAS (Updated Feb 2023).*
- **Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) - Latest Rates (Apr 2023)**:
    - Singapore Citizens (SC): 1st (0%), 2nd (20%), 3rd+ (30%).
    - Permanent Residents (PR): 1st (5%), 2nd (25%), 3rd+ (35%).
    - Foreigners: 60%.
    - Entities/Trusts: 65%.
- **Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD)**:
    - Holding period <= 1 year: 12%.
    - Holding period > 1 year and <= 2 years: 8%.
    - Holding period > 2 years and <= 3 years: 4%.
    - Holding period > 3 years: 0%.
### Mandatory Forms & Document Sequence Details

- **CEA Prescribed Estate Agency Agreements (Forms 1-8)**:
    - *Requirement*: Must be signed *before* any property is marketed or any search for property begins.
    - *Validity*: Exclusive agreements (Forms 2, 4, 6, 8) are valid for a maximum of 3 months and can be renewed.
- **Customer's Particulars Form (CPF)**:
    - *Requirement*: Mandatory for all transactions (Sale/Purchase/Lease). Used for AML/CFT compliance.
    - *Timing*: Must be completed at the point of first contact or before signing any agreement.
- **HDB Resale Sequence**:
    1. HFE Letter (Buyer) / Intent to Sell (Seller).
    2. OTP (HDB Prescribed Form with Serial Number).
    3. Valuation Request (Buyer).
    4. Resale Checklist (Both).
    5. Exercise OTP.
    6. Resale Application (Both).
- **Private Resale Sequence**:
    1. Estate Agency Agreement (Form 1/2/3/4).
    2. OTP (Law Society or Agency Template).
    3. Exercise OTP (S&P Agreement).
    4. Stamp Duty Payment (within 14 days).
- **Rental Sequence**:
    1. Estate Agency Agreement (Form 5/6/7/8).
    2. Letter of Intent (LOI) - optional but common.
    3. Tenancy Agreement (TA).
    4. Inventory List.
    5. Stamping (within 14 days).
