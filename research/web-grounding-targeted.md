### Theme 1: Anti-Money Laundering & Proliferation Financing (AML Act 2025)

**Evidence:**
The **Anti-Money Laundering and Other Matters (Estate Agents and Developers) Act 2025** (passed 8 April 2025) represents a major shift from "per case" to **"per contravention"** penalties. This means a single transaction with multiple compliance failures (e.g., failing to screen, failing to verify source of funds, and failing to keep records) can now result in cumulative fines exceeding S$300,000 for a salesperson. The Act also formally integrates **Proliferation Financing (PF)**—the funding of weapons of mass destruction—into the real estate regulatory framework.

**Best Practices:**
*   **Screening Automation:** Use CEA-approved AML/CFT screening solutions that provide real-time hits against the UN Security Council (UNSC) and FATF "Black and Grey" lists.
*   **UCPDD (Unrepresented Counterparty Due Diligence):** Since July 2025, agents must conduct due diligence on the *other* party if they are unrepresented. Best practice is to perform this concurrently with your own client's CDD to avoid transaction delays.
*   **Audit Trails:** Maintain digital "time-stamped" screenshots of screening results. Under the 2025 Act, "I checked but didn't save it" is no longer a valid defense.

**Example:**
*   *Scenario:* A salesperson facilitates a S$5M condo sale for a foreign buyer.
*   *Compliance:* The agent uses the **CEA Annex F Checklist** to screen the buyer and the unrepresented seller. They identify the buyer is from a high-risk jurisdiction and proceed to verify the **Source of Wealth (SOW)** by requesting bank statements and employment contracts before the Option to Purchase (OTP) is signed.

### Theme 2: Digital Marketing & Social Media Ethics (PC 01-24)

**Evidence:**
**Practice Circular 01-24** (issued 15 Jan 2024) specifically addresses the rise of TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts in property marketing. It mandates that the "ephemeral" nature of social media does not exempt agents from disclosure requirements.

**Best Practices:**
*   **Persistent Disclosures:** Ensure your name and CEA registration number are visible throughout the video (e.g., as a watermark) or clearly stated in the first 3 seconds.
*   **Substantiated Claims:** Avoid superlatives like "Number 1 Agent in District 9" or "Guaranteed 5% Yield" unless backed by verifiable data from the last 12 months.
*   **Owner Consent:** Always maintain a signed **Property Marketing Consent Form** before posting any "Home Tour" video.

**Example:**
*   *Scenario:* An agent posts a "Day in the Life" TikTok featuring a new luxury listing.
*   *Compliance:* The agent includes a text overlay with their **Name, Registration Number, and Agency Name**. In the caption, they explicitly state if the video is a "Sponsored Post" to comply with the Digital and Social Media Advertising Guidelines.

### Theme 3: Professional Service Standards (PC 02-24)

**Evidence:**
The **Revised Professional Service Manual (PSM)** standards under **PC 02-24** (12 Aug 2024) emphasize the "Duty of Care" to vulnerable clients (e.g., the elderly or those with limited English proficiency) and strict "No Dual Representation" rules.

**Best Practices:**
*   **Conflict Disclosure:** Use the **CEA Form 3** to declare any potential conflict of interest (e.g., if the buyer is a relative) immediately upon discovery.
*   **Standardized Templates:** Exclusively use the **SEAA/CEA Standard Tenancy Agreement** templates to ensure all statutory protections are included.
*   **Timely Documentation:** Provide copies of all signed documents to the client within **7 working days**.

**Example:**
*   *Scenario:* An agent is representing a landlord but is approached by a direct tenant.
*   *Compliance:* The agent explains they cannot represent the tenant (No Dual Representation). They provide the tenant with a "Fact Sheet for Unrepresented Parties" and conduct the mandatory **UCPDD** as required by the 2025 AML Act.

### Theme 4: High-Value Transaction Due Diligence (PC 01-25)

**Evidence:**
**PC 01-25** (20 Feb 2025) introduced **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)** triggers for residential transactions exceeding S$10 million or those involving "Complex Ownership Structures" (e.g., family trusts or offshore BVI companies).

**Best Practices:**
*   **Beneficial Ownership (BO) Mapping:** For corporate buyers, agents must "drill down" to identify the natural person who owns more than 25% of the entity.
*   **KEO Approval:** All high-value EDD reports must be reviewed and signed off by the agency’s **Key Executive Officer (KEO)** or a designated Compliance Officer.

**Example:**
*   *Scenario:* A family trust buys a Good Class Bungalow (GCB) for S$45M.
*   *Compliance:* The agent identifies the trustees and the ultimate beneficiaries. They collect a **Declaration of Source of Funds** and verify the trust deed to ensure the transaction is not an attempt to obscure the true owner.

### Theme 5: Mandatory Transaction Reporting (Notice 24-25)

**Evidence:**
**Notice 24-25** (10 June 2025) made it mandatory for all agencies to submit transaction records via the **ACEAS (Advanced CEA Estate Agencies System)** portal by the 15th of every month.

**Best Practices:**
*   **Digital Integration:** Use pre-approved digital solutions like *RealtyPromote* or *Redoc* that auto-populate transaction data into the CEA Excel template.
*   **Zero-Reporting:** Even if no deals were closed, agencies must submit a "Nil Return" to avoid administrative penalties.

**Example:**
*   *Scenario:* An agency closes 50 rental deals in March 2026.
*   *Compliance:* By **15 April 2026**, the agency’s admin uploads the bulk Excel file to ACEAS. The system validates the RES registration numbers and transaction dates against the CEA Public Register in real-time.
